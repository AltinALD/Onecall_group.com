About us

A trusted outsourcing partner, built around the phone.

To be a trusted outsourcing partner, delivering reliable and results-driven services that help companies grow.

Oncall Group is an outsourcing company providing call center services for clients in the US. Besim Kadiri founded it in Prishtina in July 2020. The floors are in Prishtina and Rahovec, and the team is 51–200 people.

Agents work on site, Monday to Friday, 15:30–23:30, so the shift lines up with the US business day. The work is customer support and telemarketing, with cold calling and lead generation in five industries.
